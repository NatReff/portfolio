Questions Before Booking

Take a few minutes to read thorugh our appointment types to know which appointment will best meet your needs.

Q. What is a Comprehensive Exam?

A “routine eye exam” takes place when you come for an eye examination without any underlying medical conditions that affect your eyes. The doctor screens the eyes and will check your vision for corrective lenses.

Q. What is a Pediatric Eye Exam?

We see children as early as one year old. We also offer a Myopia Management program for children with near-sightedness.

Q. What is a Prescription Consultation?

We can print out a copy of your eyeglass prescription at any time at your request once your exam is complete.

Q. What is a Contact Lens Fitting?

Wearing contact lenses gives patients the flexibility and freedom to live life to the fullest, without some of the difficulties presented by wearing glasses.

Q. What is Emergency Eye Care?

Eye emergencies cover a range of incidents and conditions such as; trauma, cuts, scratches, foreign objects in the eye, and blunt injuries to the eye or eyelid, etc.
**Please do not submit a contact form if you are experiencing an emergency. Please call one of our offices directly for assistance. If your emergency is life-threatening, please call 911.

Q. What is Specialized Testing?

Evaluation of a medical-related complaint or follow-up of existing conditions like Diabetes, Dry Eyes, Eye Allergies, Floaters and/or Flashes, Glaucoma, High-Risk Medications (Auto Immune, Systemic Illness Treatment), Cataracts, Macular Degeneration.
