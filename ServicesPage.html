Eye Care Services in Pensacola, FL

We believe that healthy eyesight is essential to your overall well-being, and we are here to help you achieve optimal eye health. Whether you are seeking routine eye exams, contact lenses, or treatment for an eye condition, we have the expertise and technology to meet your needs.

Comprehensive Exams

A “routine eye exam” take place when you come for an eye examination without any underlying medical conditions that affect the eyes. The doctor screens the eyes for diseases and will check your vision for corrective lenses such as glasses and/or contact lenses.

Pediatric Eye Exams

Regular eye exams are important for children since their eyes can change significantly in as little as a year as the muscles and tissue develop. Good eyesight is critical for a child’s life and achievements since success in school is closely tied to eye health.

Contact Lens Fitting

Wearing contact lenses gives patients the flexibility and freedom to live life to the fullest, without some of the difficulties presented by wearing glasses.
There are lots of different contact lenses to choose from, with two of the most popular being daily disposables and toric lenses. 

Prescription Glasses

We can print out a copy of your eyeglass prescription at any time at your request once your exam is complete. We fit distance vision only, near vision only, bifocal, trifocal, progressive, occupational, computer, anti-fatigue, safety, sports, sunglasses and recreational/hobby lenses

Emergency Eye Care

Things happen, and a sudden injury can cause pain and severe health complications.
Eye emergencies cover a range of incidents and conditions such as; trauma, cuts, scratches, foreign objects in the eye, burns, chemical exposure, photic retinopathy, and blunt injuries to the eye or eyelid.

Specialized Testing

We offer Optomap, Ocular Coherence Tomography (OCT), Visual Field, MacuLogix, LipiFlow® and ForeseeHome (Home AMD monitoring) at all locations.
